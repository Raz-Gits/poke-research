# Set Projections — 2026-06-22

**What this is:** a forward read on the 11 watched sealed ETBs, built by combining
**our own price data** (TCGplayer sealed market history, backfilled from the local
tcgcsv archive — 789 weekly points back to 2024) with a **forward-fundamentals
pass** (print run / rotation / reprint status — see [SET_OUTLOOK_2026-06-22.md](SET_OUTLOOK_2026-06-22.md)).

**Honest caveat up front (important):** our momentum numbers are derived from
TCGplayer **"market"** price, which — per both the Codex review and
[MARKET_RESEARCH_2026-06-22.md](MARKET_RESEARCH_2026-06-22.md) — tends to sit
*above* realized eBay **sold** and can be noisy for thin sealed products. So momentum
direction is trustworthy when it's a smooth multi-week move; absolute price *levels*
should be sanity-checked against the eBay floor (e.g. our 151 std "market" reads ~$599
while real eBay BINs are ~$460–510). Momentum alone is also **reprint-blind** — a set
can climb right up until a reprint lands. That's exactly why this report pairs momentum
with the print/reprint fundamentals rather than trusting either alone.

## Does our data agree with the ones you added?

**151 + 151 PC — yes, strongly (this is the highest-conviction pair you own).**
- **151 PC** is the standout: our data shows it rock-steady (~$1,360–1,400 for 10 straight weeks, +62% / 6mo) and the fundamentals are the best in the set — genuinely out-of-print, *no booster box was ever made*, and the stamped-Snorlax promo is a durable moat. Data and fundamentals fully agree.
- **151 std** has the same out-of-print scarcity thesis, but our price data flags a nuance the fundamentals miss: it's **volatile and currently cooling** — it spiked to ~$675 in early May then drifted to ~$599 (−9% / 30d). So it's a great long-term hold, but right now you'd be **buying into a pullback, not a bounce**. Patience / buying weakness beats chasing. And keep your eBay cap honest — our "market" reads ~$599 but real BINs are ~$460–510 (you're already capping at $510, which is right).

**Surging Sparks — our *price* data backs it, but the fundamentals add a real caveat.**
- Our data is unambiguous and **not an artifact**: the standard ETB has climbed smoothly from ~$102 → $136 over nine straight weeks (+21% / 30d, +99% / 6mo). That's a genuine uptrend.
- **But** the outlook research confirms Surging Sparks was **reprinted in 2026** and stays Standard-legal — so unlike 151, there's no out-of-print scarcity, and fresh reprints can cap or reverse the run. Verdict: the momentum agreeing with your buy is real *for now*, but this is a **momentum hold with a reprint ceiling, not a scarcity hold**. Lower conviction than 151. (PC Surging Sparks is just flat — neutral.)

**Pitch Black (presale) — can't validate; buy at MSRP ($59.99) only.** That $577 in our data is a thin TCGplayer presale artifact, not real. The fundamentals say PC ETBs of *normal* sets (which this is — it has a booster box) grind modestly up post-release but face restock dips. Reasonable at MSRP, risky at a big presale premium.

## The meta-lesson (and why these sometimes disagreed)
On Surging Sparks our momentum said "hot" while the fundamentals said "reprinted/soft." Neither is wrong — they measure different things. **Momentum (TCGplayer market) catches the move; fundamentals (reprint/OOP) tell you whether the move is durable.** A projection that trusts only momentum will keep recommending reprinted sets right up until supply lands. That's the same root issue Codex and the market-research audit flagged: our pricing leans on TCGplayer "market," which runs ahead of realized sold and is blind to supply. The durable fix is to anchor signals to **sold + scarcity**, which is the direction worth building next.
